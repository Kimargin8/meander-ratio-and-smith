## OEIS input data

The file [b005315.txt](ratio/b005315.txt) reproduces the table of orders 0–38 from *The On-Line Encyclopedia of Integer Sequences*, OEIS Foundation Inc. and its contributors. The snapshot was retrieved on 15 November 2026. Its bytes are unchanged. The entry credits Efthimios Alepis for the table, with earlier values from Iwan Jensen, Andrew Howroyd and Vladimir Osipov. The occupation tables and related scalar checks in the ratio directory use these published counts as inputs.
